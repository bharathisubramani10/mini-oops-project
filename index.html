Life Cycle of a Grievance

From submission to multi-tier escalation and final resolution, every transition is backed by clean Object-Oriented state management.

1. Ticket Lodged

Complainant logs grievance with category, description, and evidence. Triggers ComplaintFiledEvent.

2. SLA Assignment

Daemon thread schedules SLA based on priority using a PriorityQueue comparator algorithm.

3. Polymorphic Escalation

Unresolved tickets polymorphically escalate to higher executive authorities (Dean, Warden, CTO).

Escalated
4. Redressal & Closure

Officer inputs resolution notes, notifies complainant via background thread, and archives ticket.
